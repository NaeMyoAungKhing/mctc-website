About MCTC

A college that works like a living system

Founded in Yangon in February 2017. A Pearson BTEC centre for digital art, game development, animation and motion graphics — built to train digital artists and place them in real studios. We teach by intuition, inspiration and innovation.

Story

Since 2017

Started by artists for artists, with one idea: if you love something, make it your career.

Standards

Pearson BTEC International Centre 92672

We run the full Pearson quality cycle: internal verification of every brief and of marked work, and annual Standards Verification by Pearson. Your qualification is the same one awarded in the UK.

Partners

The coalition

Nestlé, MSI, Wacom, Bandai Namco, NVIDIA, Ooredoo, ViewSonic, Pearson.

People

Faculty & leadership

Practising artists, art directors and alumni.

Campus

Three studios, six days a week

Rooms A, B and C in Botahtaung, Yangon. Classes run Monday to Saturday in morning, afternoon and evening slots, so students can study around work.
