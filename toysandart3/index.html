Where art, figures and cosplay come to life

Toys & Art is Yangon's festival of Japanese-inspired creativity — figures and dioramas, anime and manga art, cosplay and anisong — made by the students and lecturers of Myanmar Creative Technology College with partners across the region. Vol.2 in May 2025 drew thousands to Junction City. Vol.3 goes bigger.

What's on

Four stages of the weekend. Two confirmed, two we're still building — follow @toysandart.mm for each reveal.

Activity 01 / 04

Toys & figures showcase

100+ handcrafted dioramas bringing anime and gaming scenes to life, shown beside authentic Japanese figures. All made by MCTC students and lecturers.

Activity 02 / 04

Cosplay contest

Local and international cosplayers on stage, Yangon's largest prize pool, and every MCTC student in character for the whole event.

TentativeActivity 03 / 04

Gaming arena

Bracket play, free stations and a community corner for the gaming side of the fandom.

TentativeActivity 04 / 04

Live anime music finale

Finale night at the Melia Hotel ballroom: a live anisong band closes the weekend.

Also on the floorCharacter wall

30–40 anime and manga characters drawn live on large PVC canvases by MCTC artists and guest illustrators.

Also on the floorQuests

Visit booths, complete sponsor quests, collect stickers. A gamified route through the whole event.

Also on the floorLive editing

Real-time photo and video editing demos — anime-style filters and effects by the MCTC team.

Also on the floorDrawing contest

Open drawing contest and live crafting demonstrations across the weekend.

New for Vol.3

Merch, but make it gacha

Instead of a shop counter, student-made merchandise comes out of a gacha machine. Buy or earn tickets, press summon, watch the pull animation, and collect what drops — every item designed by an MCTC student.
