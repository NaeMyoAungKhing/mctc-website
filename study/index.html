Study at MCTC

UK-accredited programmes, Yangon practice.

One ladder, Pearson BTEC all the way: Level 3 Foundation, two HND years, and a degree top-up in development. Taught through live briefs, not lectures alone.

The ladder

Four levels, one path

Level 3

Foundation Diploma in Art & Design

The entry year. Three units: Skill Development (graphic design, drawing fundamentals, writing & authenticity), Responding to a Brief (a Pearson-set 90-hour brief) and a Creative Project capstone that carries you into HND. Nine months to one year full-time, accepted as the equivalent of A-Level entry, with new batches opening through the year.

HND Level 4

Year 1 — Foundation of Creative Practice

Five industry-style briefs: Ways of Seeing, Cultural Sustainability, Telling Tales, TV Logo & Ident, How It’s Done. Every brief runs the same five strands — context, ideas, technical, professional, communication — so you learn to work like a studio.

HND Level 5

Year 2 — Professional & Applied Practice

Personal professional development, a Pearson-set professional project, and a practical application block alongside — the year your portfolio becomes a career.

Level 6

Degree top-up — in development

A third year leading to a degree, with university partnerships now being formed. Ask admissions for the latest.

How we teach

Three studios, Monday to Saturday — or online

Morning (10–12), afternoon (13–16) and evening (19–21) slots in rooms A, B and C, blended with online teaching on Discord so students outside Yangon can join. Small cohorts, live briefs, internal verification at every stage and annual Pearson Standards Verification — Centre 92672.
